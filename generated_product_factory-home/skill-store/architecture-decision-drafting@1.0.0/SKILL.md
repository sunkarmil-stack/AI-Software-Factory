# architecture-decision-drafting

Draft architecture decision records with context, options, decision, consequences, evidence and revisit triggers.

## Inputs
- spec
- existing ADRs

## Outputs
- ADR drafts with status proposed

## Procedure
1. List the forces and constraints from NFRs and data classification.
2. Give at least two options with trade-offs.
3. Record evidence (spike results, benchmarks) or mark the decision unresolved.
4. State revisit triggers that would reopen the decision.

## Limitations
- Drafts only; the lead architect approves.

Requested capabilities are requests only; the runtime identity and policy decide what is granted.
