# Retail operations glossary (synthetic)

An account is a deposit or credit arrangement held by a customer within one tenant, recording a balance and currency.

A hold (also called a freeze) is a temporary restriction placed on part of an account balance for a stated amount and reason.

Holds above 10000 dollars require supervisor approval.

A ledger entry (also called a posting) is an immutable record of a debit or credit.
